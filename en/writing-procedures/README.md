A procedure is read by somebody who is in the middle of doing something, often in a hurry, sometimes
under pressure. That reader is not browsing.

| Page | Answers |
| :--- | :--- |
| [The shape of a procedure](the-shape-of-a-procedure.md) | What every procedure contains, in what order |
| [Steps, warnings and safety](steps-warnings-and-safety.md) | Writing an instruction somebody can follow |
| [Reuse](reuse.md) | Saying it once and showing it everywhere |

> [!NOTE]
> **Write for the worst moment, not the training session**
>
> The page that reads well at a desk can be unusable at 3am with gloves on. If a procedure will ever
> be followed under pressure, that is the condition to write for.
